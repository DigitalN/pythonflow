Privacy

  • Powerflow never connects to the internet: no update checks, analytics, or crash reporting, and no local server or open ports.
  • It only reads power sensors (the battery registry and SMC). It doesn't read serial numbers or your computer's name.
  • Charging history and settings stay in ~/Library/Application Support/Powerflow.
